# SRS-311.1 — View officials list (AO)

> **SRS-311.1, AO, View officials list.** The system shall display the officials found by the signed-in user's saved extractions who have an email address or telephone number, each listed once with the documents they were found in.
>
> **Acceptance:**
> * Each official appears once in the list, even when several of the user's extractions found them.
> * Each entry shows name, title, role, email address, and telephone number, with the most recent details found.
> * Only officials with an email address or telephone number are listed.
> * Details that aren't available are labeled as unavailable (consistent with UR-308).
> * Each entry links to every source document it was found in (consistent with UR-303).
> * Officials from another user's extractions are not listed.
>
> Traces to: UR-311

**Changed during Sprint 3 (Task 311.1-6):** this item first read "a single shared list of officials extracted from documents for the pilot municipality". After trying the page, the Officials tab is meant to be the user's own contacts: built from their saved extractions, people with contact details only. The SRS was reworded to match, and the change is noted on the issue.

## How it works
`GET /api/officials` reads the signed-in user's **completed** extractions and their officials (`extracted_officials`), oldest first, and builds the list (`lib/officialsList.ts`):

1. The same name (ignoring letter case and extra spaces) in the same town is **one entry**, as in SRS-311.2.
2. A later extraction's details win; a detail it didn't find keeps the earlier value.
3. Only people with an **email or telephone number** are kept.
4. Each entry lists every **document** it was found in, linking to the document's page.

Deleting a saved extraction (SRS-305.4) removes what it contributed, since the list is built from the user's extractions each time.

## Demo

1. Log in. On **Contacts → Request**, extract the **Joint Public Hearing with the Planning Board Monday October 19, 2026** agenda: Maureen Elizabeth Fisher, City Clerk, no contact details.
2. Open the **Officials** tab: she isn't listed yet (no email or phone).
3. Extract the **Salem City Council Special Meeting October 15, 2026** agenda: it gives her email and phone.
4. Open **Officials**: Maureen Elizabeth Fisher appears **once**, as **Acting City Clerk** with **mfisher@salem.com** and **978-745-9595 ext. 41200**, and **Found in** lists both documents. *(Acceptance criteria.)*
5. Delete both on the **Saved** tab: the Officials tab is empty again.
